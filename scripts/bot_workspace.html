Setup guide & how actions work
  1. Choose a bot and use the suggested routine or enter your own assignment.
  2. Assign one task now, or choose an interval and save the routine.
  3. Review findings in Reports. Use “Get action options” for a concrete decision.
  4. Choose a strategy in Overview. The bot remembers it and prepares an implementation brief.
  5. Request a repair from an active problem card. The Operator keeps its existing tests, scope limits and rollback checks.

Provider fallback: Codex → Claude → local Qwen, subject to login and availability. Routines continue with this page closed while the laptop and dashboard service run. Channel publishing schedules remain separate.
