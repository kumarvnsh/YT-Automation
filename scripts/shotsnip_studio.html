HOW IT WORKS

You choose the moment.

  1. Find it

    Use automatic discovery, a live URL, a replay, or exact moments.

  2. Finish it

    We cut, caption and apply your large-gameplay template.

  3. Review or release

    Watch the preview in Work queue. Publish to your selected platforms when ready.

The automatic flow stays on.

Manual jobs take the next free slot. Manual publications share the configured posting window and reset its timer; previews do not.

Replay chat and live DVR are best effort. When a source cannot provide the requested interval, the job shows a clear failure instead of publishing a different moment.

    HOW TO READ IT

    Queue rules

    1. One at a time. A running job finishes its current stage before anything else starts.
    2. Manual first. Waiting manual jobs run before the next automatic cycle. Use Move to front to reorder them.
    3. Cycles can be skipped. If no stream has a good enough moment, that cycle posts nothing and the next one tries again.
    4. Uncertain uploads hold publishing. If YouTube didn't confirm an upload, nothing else publishes until it's checked.
